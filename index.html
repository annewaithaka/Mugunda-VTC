Our story

From adult education to vocational training

Founded in May 2023 as an adult education centre in response to community demand, Mugunda has grown into a fully TVET-accredited vocational training institution serving Nyeri County's Kieni West Sub-County.

Adult learners still prepare for KCSE with us, and school leavers now gain hands-on vocational skills in cookery, computing, and caregiving — all under one roof.
