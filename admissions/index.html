Start your training with us

Enrolment at Mugunda is straightforward. Choose the programme that fits you, reach out to our team, and we'll guide you through the rest.

How to enrol

Three steps to get started

1

Choose your programme

Browse our six programmes — from Food & Beverage Production to Home-based Care, computing, and adult education. Pick the one that matches your goals.

2

Get in touch

Call, WhatsApp, email, or fill in the enquiry form below. We'll answer your questions, confirm entry requirements, and let you know when the next intake starts.

3

Complete enrolment

Visit our campus at Nairutia Shopping Centre with your documents and registration fee. Our team will help you complete the paperwork and welcome you to Mugunda.

What to prepare

Documents to bring

To make enrolment quick, please have these ready when you visit or reach out.

  • Kenyan National ID or Birth Certificate Original plus a copy for our records
  • Two passport-size photos Recent, plain background
  • Academic certificates (where applicable) KCSE or KCPE results — required for some programmes
  • Registration fee Fees vary by programme — contact us for the current amount

Requirements may vary by programme. If you're not sure what applies to you, message us on WhatsApp and we'll walk you through it.
